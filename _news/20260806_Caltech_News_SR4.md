[Caltech News](https://www.caltech.edu/about/news/new-quantum-microscopy-trick-quadruples-microscope-resolution) features our work on quantum microscopy in “New Quantum Microscopy Trick Quadruples Microscope Resolution.”

Using entangled photon pairs and multiple passes in the object-free idler arm, we demonstrated a fourfold resolution improvement over classical imaging while the signal photon passes through the sample only once.

The paper, [“Above-twofold quantum super-resolution microscopy enabled by multiple idler passes with entangled biphotons”](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.aea9457), was published in _Science Advances_ on July 24, 2026.
